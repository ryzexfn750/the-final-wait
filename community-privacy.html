Community Privacy Notice

Draft updated: 29 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-29

Draft — community registrations are not yet available. The Final Wait is the public name of this independent fan project. Before enabling accounts, the legal identity of the person or entity responsible for processing personal data must be confirmed and disclosed as required by Article 13 GDPR. This notice must also be reviewed against the actual service-provider arrangements and retention settings.

1. Project and contact

Public project name: The Final Wait (thefinalwait.com). For privacy questions, requests, or account assistance, contact support@thefinalwait.com. This email address is a contact channel; the project name alone is not represented as the verified legal identity of the data controller.

2. Information we handle

If you choose to join the community, the service may handle your Google-authenticated email address, authentication identifier, chosen public nickname, the version and time of your acceptance of community terms, your optional newsletter preference and consent timestamp, your selected time zone, and temporary information needed for live reactions. Google authentication and the hosting/security providers may separately process technical connection and security information in line with their own policies.

We do not ask community members to publish their real name. Your email address is not displayed to other community members; your nickname and live emoji reactions may be visible to participants in the same time-zone room. Every registered account also receives a collectible boarding pass showing its public nickname, edition, ticket number and original registration date. Anyone holding your unguessable public verification link can view these details. The link is not intended for indexing and will stop working after account deletion. Avoid sharing it if you do not want this information visible to recipients.

3. Why the information is used

Authentication, account management and live reactions are processed to provide the community service you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where applicable). Security and abuse prevention may rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)), subject to the required balancing assessment. Optional announcement emails require a separate, affirmative choice (consent, Article 6(1)(a)). Refusing newsletter consent does not prevent access to the community.

4. Services involved

Supabase is used for Google sign-in integration and account/profile storage; Google provides authentication; Cloudflare hosts the site, security checks and live-reaction infrastructure; Sender manages the optional announcement mailing list. These services may process personal data for their functions. Their contracts, processing locations, retention and any international transfer safeguards must be reviewed before public launch. No specific storage region or transfer mechanism is guaranteed by this draft.

5. Newsletters and withdrawal

Joining the mailing list is optional. When you opt in, your email address is sent to Sender for The Final Wait announcements. You may withdraw consent using the unsubscribe link included in newsletters or by contacting support@thefinalwait.com. Unsubscribing does not require deleting your community account. Requests that fail to synchronize with the mailing provider need manual resolution before any further email is sent to that address.

6. Retention

Account and profile records are normally retained while your account is active and are intended to be removed following account deletion, subject to necessary security, legal, and backup-retention requirements. Live reaction data is designed to be short-lived. Newsletter information is retained while you are subscribed and as necessary to document consent or honor opt-out requests. Exact provider backup, log and deletion periods remain to be verified before launch.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing; you may withdraw any newsletter consent at any time. Email support@thefinalwait.com. You may also complain to the competent data-protection authority, including the Italian Data Protection Authority where applicable.

8. Website analytics and changes

Optional Google Analytics cookies are used only after affirmative consent through separate ACCEPT/DECLINE controls. They are not required for account registration, Terms acceptance or newsletter subscriptions. Refusing does not limit site access. This notice will be updated before public registration and whenever significant processing changes are introduced.
