Draft updated: 29 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-29

Draft — not yet valid for public registrations. The Final Wait is the project's public name. The legally responsible operator must be identified and the terms reviewed before public accounts are enabled.

1. Independent fan project

The Final Wait is an independent, unofficial fan website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and related names, trademarks and official imagery belong to their respective owners.

2. Community access

In the current community preview, you must sign in with Google and choose a public nickname to access the website. Your email is not published to other participants. Do not use a nickname that impersonates a person, a moderator, Rockstar Games, or the site operator. Do not attempt to bypass authentication or security checks. Each registered account is assigned one decorative, non-transferable The Final Wait Airways collectible. It is not a real boarding pass, travel booking, Rockstar product or proof of identity. A shared verification link displays your nickname and registration date. Deleting your account also invalidates its ticket link.

3. Live reactions

Live Hype lets participants send emoji reactions to a time-zone room. Reactions may be shown alongside a nickname. Automated traffic, spam, harassment, security abuse, and attempts to overload or disrupt the service are prohibited. Reactions may be rate-limited, delayed, or unavailable during periods of high traffic.

4. Optional announcement emails

Receiving newsletter emails is entirely optional and requires a separate affirmative choice. You can withdraw that choice without losing your community account, using an unsubscribe link or by contacting support@thefinalwait.com.

5. Account controls and privacy

Your chosen nickname is permanent after registration. You may view your profile, manage optional email preferences and request account deletion through the available account controls. If a linked mailing-list removal fails, additional support may be required to complete account deletion safely. See the Community Privacy Notice for details about personal-data handling and your rights.

6. Operation and changes

The community may be moderated, temporarily limited or suspended when needed for security and service reliability. Live reactions are provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability. The main countdown and music are separate from the community feature. Material changes to these terms will be communicated where required.

7. Contact

Contact: support@thefinalwait.com. The operator's legally required identifying details remain to be finalized before public registration opens.
