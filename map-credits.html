Geographic data

Time zone boundaries come from Simplified Timezone Boundaries, derived from Timezone Boundary Builder and OpenStreetMap contributors. The geographic data is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Boundaries are simplified and may omit small islands.

The visualization uses Globe.GL, Three.js and an Earth image from the Three-Globe example assets. The JavaScript libraries are available under the MIT licence.

Midnight countdown

Regions are illuminated when their local clock reaches 00:00 on November 19, 2026, using IANA time zones. This fan-made visualization does not represent Rockstar Games' official release or platform unlock times.
