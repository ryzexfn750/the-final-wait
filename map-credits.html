Map Data & Credits

Sources and licenses behind the interactive world map.

Geographic data

Time zone boundaries come from Simplified Timezone Boundaries, derived from Timezone Boundary Builder and OpenStreetMap contributors. The geographic data is licensed under Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Boundaries are simplified.

3D globe

Powered by Globe.GL and Three.js (MIT open-source libraries). The night-Earth image comes from Three-Globe's example assets.

About the countdown

The map visualizes midnight on November 19, 2026 in each IANA time zone. It is a fan-made experience and does not represent Rockstar Games' official release or platform unlock times.
