Open-source projects, geographic data and licenses behind the interactive world map.

01

GEOGRAPHIC DATA

Time zone polygons are sourced from Simplified Timezone Boundaries, derived from Timezone Boundary Builder and OpenStreetMap contributors. The site hosts a compact GeoJSON copy containing 406 simplified source regions. Small islands and boundary details may be omitted.

02

3D GLOBE & RENDERING

The interactive Earth is powered by Globe.GL and Three.js. The night-Earth image is loaded from the Three-Globe example assets. The colorful midnight wave, timeline, region interaction and 2D fallback are custom features of The Final Wait.

03

LOCAL MIDNIGHT CALCULATIONS

Each region is illuminated when its IANA time zone reaches 00:00 on November 19, 2026. The website uses browser internationalization rules to calculate local midnight and account for applicable UTC offsets and daylight saving time.

This is a fan-made countdown visualization, not a statement of Rockstar Games' official release or platform unlock schedule. The map is illustrative, not a legally authoritative geographic or time zone reference.
